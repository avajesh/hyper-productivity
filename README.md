## 🚀 Welcome to Hyper Productivity!

**Hyper Productivity** is an evolution of the widely popular [Super Productivity](https://github.com/super-productivity/super-productivity) open-source app, supercharged with community-requested features, a refreshed modern UI, and unlimited organizational flexibility. 

Whether you are managing complex software projects, studying, or just organizing your day, Hyper Productivity gives you the absolute best tools for the job—100% free and open-source.

## ✨ What makes Hyper Productivity better?

- **Infinite Task Hierarchies (Nested Subtasks):** The #1 most requested feature by the productivity community is here. You are no longer restricted to a flat list of subtasks. Break your work down into subtasks, sub-sub-tasks, and sub-sub-sub-tasks!
- **Recursive Time Tracking & Rollups:** Tracking time is no longer restricted to simple tasks! When you log time or set estimates on a deep sub-sub-task, those numbers automatically bubble up and aggregate at every parent level. You get a perfect, real-time picture of exactly how much time an entire massive project or feature is taking.
- **Modernized UI:** Updated color palettes (Emerald/Deep Teal) for better focus and less eye strain.
- **Privacy First, Always:** No tracking, no data mining, and everything is stored locally on your device by default. Cloud sync is completely optional.

## 💡 Features

* **Plan, track & summarize:** Create time-sheets and work summaries in a breeze to easily export them to your company's time-tracking system.
* **Integrate with Jira, GitHub, GitLab and others:** Auto-import tasks assigned to you, plan the details locally, and automatically submit work logs back with one click.
* **Organize your project data:** Link local files, bookmarks, emails, and external documents directly to tasks so you don't have to search for them later.
* **Healthy working habits:** A built-in pomodoro timer and break reminders to help you stay focused and healthy.
* **100% Free and Open Source:** No accounts, no registration, no hidden paywalls.
